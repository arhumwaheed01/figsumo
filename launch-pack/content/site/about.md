## What we do differently

- **Country-correct results.** Tax, payroll, mortgage and savings rules differ between the US, Canada and the UK. Our country calculators use each government's published figures, and they show the tax year those figures apply to.
- **We show the working.** Every calculator page explains the formula, works through at least one example step by step, and lists the official sources we used.
- **Dated and reviewed.** Each page shows when it was last updated. Tax and rate figures are checked again whenever the government publishes new ones. See our [Methodology](/methodology).
- **Your inputs stay on your device.** Calculations run in your browser. We don't ask you to create an account. **[CONFIRM after DEV-TASKS T-229.]** See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
