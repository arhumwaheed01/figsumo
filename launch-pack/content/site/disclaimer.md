# Disclaimer

## General
FigSumo's calculators and articles are for general information, education and planning. Results are **estimates** based on the inputs you enter, the formulas described on each page, and the assumptions stated with each result.

## Not professional advice
Nothing on FigSumo is financial, investment, tax, accounting, legal or medical advice, and nothing on the site is a recommendation to buy, sell or do anything. Using the site does not create a professional–client relationship. For decisions that matter, consult a qualified professional who knows your circumstances.

## Financial and tax calculators
- Tax and payroll calculators use figures published by the IRS, SSA, CRA, HMRC and other authorities for the tax year shown on each page. They don't cover every rule, credit, deduction or regional variation; each page lists what is not included.
- Loan, mortgage and savings results are not offers of credit or deposit products. Your lender's or provider's documents (for example a US Loan Estimate, a UK ESIS, or a Canadian cost-of-borrowing disclosure) are the official figures.
- Investment projections assume constant returns. They are hypothetical, not forecasts. The value of investments can go down as well as up, and you may get back less than you invest.

## Health calculators
Health calculators (for example BMI, calorie, BMR, body fat and due date) use published equations and classifications. They can't diagnose any condition or replace a consultation with a doctor, midwife, dietitian or other healthcare professional. If you have a medical concern, contact a healthcare provider. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
