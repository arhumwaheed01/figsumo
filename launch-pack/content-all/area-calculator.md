H1: Area Calculator
Area is the amount of flat space inside a shape, measured in square units such as square feet (ft²) or square metres (m²). You need it to buy flooring, paint, turf, fabric or fertiliser, and for geometry homework. This calculator finds the area of four common shapes.
How to use this calculator
1. Shape: rectangle, triangle, trapezoid or circle.
2. Dimension 1, 2 and 3, which depend on the shape:
Rectangle: dimension 1 = length, dimension 2 = width
Triangle: dimension 1 = base, dimension 2 = perpendicular height
Trapezoid: dimension 1 = bottom base, dimension 2 = height, dimension 3 = top base
Circle: dimension 1 = radius
3. Use the same unit for every dimension.
Result: area (to four decimal places) in the square of your unit, with the formula used.

Worked examples
Example 1: rectangle 10 × 6
A = 10 × 6 = 60 square units.
Example 2: triangle, base 10, height 6
A = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30. A triangle is half of the rectangle around it.
Example 3: trapezoid, bases 10 and 4, height 6
A = ½ × (10 + 4) × 6 = 42.
Example 4: circle, radius 10
A = π × 10² = 314.1593. For a circle 6 across, the radius is 3, so A = 28.2743.
Real-world measuring
Rooms: a 12 ft × 15 ft room is 180 ft², or 16.72 m². For an L-shaped room, split it into rectangles, find each area and add them.
Metric to imperial: 1 m² = 10.7639 ft²; 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m² exactly. A 4.5 m × 3.6 m room is 16.2 m², or 174.38 ft².
Area vs perimeter: area is the space inside; perimeter is the distance around. The 12 × 15 ft room has a perimeter of 66 ft, which is what you'd need for skirting boards.

A 90° sector of a circle with radius 10 is a quarter of the circle: 314.1593 ÷ 4 ≈ 78.54. A parallelogram with base 10 and height 6 has the same area as the 10 × 6 rectangle, 60, because sliding the slanted part across makes a rectangle.

Tips and common mistakes
Using the slanted side of a triangle instead of the perpendicular height.
Using diameter as radius for circles; it gives four times the right area.
Mixing units, such as feet and inches. Convert first: 6 in = 0.5 ft.
Forgetting waste when buying materials. Add 5–15% for cutting, depending on the material.
Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate area?
Multiply length by width for a rectangle; other shapes use the formulas above.
How do I find the area of an irregular shape?
Divide it into rectangles, triangles and circles, then add the pieces.
