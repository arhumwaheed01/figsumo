H1: Sales Tax Calculator
In the United States, sales tax is added at the register on top of the shelf price, and the rate depends on where you buy: the state rate plus any county, city or special-district rates. This calculator adds sales tax to a pre-tax price and shows the tax and the total you'll pay.
How to use this calculator
1. Price before tax: the listed price of the item or the total of your purchase.
2. Sales tax rate (%): the combined rate for the place of sale. Your receipt, the state revenue department's rate lookup, or the retailer's checkout page will show it.
Results: sales tax and total price.
The formula
Sales tax = Price × Rate ÷ 100
Total = Price + Sales tax
To work backwards from a total that already includes tax:
Pre-tax price = Total ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100)
Tax included = Total − Pre-tax price
To find the rate from a receipt: Rate = Tax ÷ Pre-tax price × 100.
Worked examples
Example 1: $99.99 at 8.25%
Tax = 99.99 × 0.0825 = $8.25
Total = $108.24
Example 2: working back from a receipt total
You paid $54.12 including 7.25% tax.
Pre-tax price = 54.12 ÷ 1.0725 = $50.46
Tax included = $3.66
Example 3: combining state and local rates
A 6% state rate plus 2.25% local rates gives a combined rate of 8.25%. On a $1,250 purchase, that's $103.12 in tax and $1,353.12 in total.
Example 4: finding the rate
If a $40.00 purchase shows $3.30 tax, the rate is 8.25%.
Example 5: the same $1,500 laptop in different places
Oregon (no sales tax): $1,500.00
At California's 7.25% statewide base rate: $1,608.75
At a 10.25% combined rate found in some California cities: $1,653.75
The $153.75 spread is why the delivery address matters for online orders.

Understanding your results
Discounts come off before tax in most cases: a $100 item marked down to $80 at 8.25% costs $86.60 including $6.60 tax. Manufacturer coupons can be treated differently from store coupons in some states.
Rounding: retailers round the tax to the nearest cent, usually per transaction; occasional one-cent differences from the calculator are normal.
What's taxable varies. Many states exempt or reduce tax on groceries and prescription drugs; clothing is exempt in some states; services are taxed in some states and not others.
How US sales tax works
No national sales tax. Sales tax is set by states and local governments.
Five states have no statewide sales tax: Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon. Alaska allows local sales taxes.
Highest state rate: California's statewide base rate is 7.25%, and local add-ons push combined rates higher in many areas. Combined state and local rates in some localities exceed 10% [VERIFY current highest combined rates].
Use tax: if you buy from an out-of-state seller that doesn't collect tax, you may owe use tax at your home state's rate. Since the 2018 South Dakota v. Wayfair Supreme Court decision, states can require large online sellers to collect tax even without a physical presence.
Vehicles: many states tax a car on the price after the trade-in value is deducted. At 6%, tax on a $30,000 car is $1,800.00 on the full price or $1,320.00 after an $8,000 trade-in, depending on the state rule. See the auto loan calculator.
Federal deduction: if you itemize, you can deduct state and local general sales taxes instead of state income tax, within the SALT cap [VERIFY 2026 SALT cap and phase-out]. The IRS has a sales tax deduction calculator.
Canada and the UK
Canada and the UK use value-added style taxes. In Canada, GST (5%) or HST (combined federal–provincial) applies, with provincial sales tax charged separately in British Columbia, Saskatchewan, Manitoba and Quebec; see the GST/HST calculator. In the UK, prices shown to consumers include VAT, normally 20%; see the VAT calculator.
Tips and common mistakes
Using only the state rate. Add local rates for the actual delivery or purchase address.
Applying tax to the wrong amount. Shipping and handling are taxable in some states and not others.
Dividing by the rate to reverse. To remove tax, divide by (1 + rate), not multiply by (1 − rate).
Assuming online purchases are tax-free. Most large retailers now collect tax.
Keeping records
If you itemize deductions, you can deduct either state income tax or state and local sales tax, not both. Residents of states with no income tax often choose sales tax. You can use the IRS optional sales tax tables or your actual receipts, plus the tax on big purchases such as a car. Keep receipts for large items, since the calculator's figures help you check what you paid. [VERIFY]
Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate sales tax?
Multiply the price by the rate (as a decimal), then add it to the price.
How do I remove sales tax from a total?
Divide the total by 1 plus the rate. At 8%, divide by 1.08.
Which states have no sales tax?
Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon have no statewide sales tax.
Is sales tax charged on shipping?
It depends on the state and whether shipping is separately stated.
Can I deduct sales tax on my federal return?
Yes, if you itemize and choose sales tax instead of state income tax, subject to the SALT limit.
Why is my rate different from my neighbour's?
Local rates vary by city, county and special district, so addresses near each other can have different combined rates.
