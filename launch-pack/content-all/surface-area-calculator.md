H1: Surface Area Calculator
Surface area is the total area of all the faces or curved surfaces of a 3D shape. You need it to estimate paint, wrapping, insulation, sheet metal or labels. Choose a shape, enter its measurements, and this calculator gives the surface area in square units.

The formulas
Shape
Surface area
Cube
6a²
Rectangular prism
2(lw + lh + wh)
Sphere
4πr²
Closed cylinder
2πr(r + h) = 2πr² + 2πrh
The cylinder formula is two circular ends (2πr²) plus the curved side, which unrolls into a rectangle of width 2πr (the circumference) and height h.
Worked examples
Example 1: cube with 3 cm edges
6 × 3² = 54 cm²
Example 2: box 3 × 4 × 5
2(12 + 15 + 20) = 94 square units
Example 3: sphere of radius 3
4π × 9 = 113.0973
Example 4: cylinder, radius 3, height 4
2π × 3 × (3 + 4) = 131.9469
Example 5: painting a room
A room 4 m × 3 m with 2.5 m walls has a total box surface of 59.0 m². Subtract floor and ceiling (2 × 12 m²) for the walls alone: 35.0 m², before deducting doors and windows. For coverage, check the paint tin (often around 10–12 m² per litre per coat) [VERIFY per product].
Example 6: a food can label
A can 7.5 cm across (r = 3.75) and 11 cm tall has a total surface of about 347.5 cm²; the label alone is the curved side, 2πrh.
Example 7: a basketball
A size-7 ball with a radius of about 11.05 cm has a surface area of roughly 1,534 cm².
Surface area vs volume
Surface area is measured in square units (cm², ft²); volume in cubic units (cm³, ft³). As an object grows, its volume increases faster than its surface: double a cube's edge and its surface area quadruples while its volume rises eightfold. That's why small animals lose heat faster and why crushed ice melts sooner than a block. See the volume calculator.
Understanding your results
Open containers: for an open-topped box or cylinder, subtract the missing face (lw for the box top, πr² for a cylinder end).
Diameter vs radius: halve the diameter before entering it.
Converting units: 1 m² = 10,000 cm²; 1 ft² = 144 in². The unit converter helps.
Tips and common mistakes
Entering a diameter as the radius, which quadruples the sphere's area.
Forgetting the ends of a cylinder or including them when they aren't there.
Mixing units, such as feet for length and inches for height.
Confusing surface area with volume when buying materials.
Notes for US, Canada and UK readers
US paint and siding coverage is quoted in square feet per gallon; UK and Canadian tins use m² per litre, so convert the surface area before you buy.
Frequently asked questions
What is the surface area of a cube?
Six times the edge squared.
How do I find the surface area of a cylinder?
2πr² for the ends plus 2πrh for the side.
What is the surface area of a sphere?
4πr².
Are the units squared?
Yes: square centimetres, square feet and so on.
How do I calculate an open box?
Use lw + 2lh + 2wh (one base, no top).
Can I calculate a cone?
Not yet. Use πr(r + s), where s is the slant height √(r² + h²).
