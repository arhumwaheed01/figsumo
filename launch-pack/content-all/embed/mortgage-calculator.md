H1: Free Mortgage Calculator Widget
If you run a real estate, mortgage broker, home builder or personal finance website, a mortgage calculator gives visitors a reason to stay on your page. This page lets you preview FigSumo's mortgage payment calculator and copy a single line of code to place it on your own site, free.
How to use the embed generator
1. Loan amount, interest rate (APR %) and loan term (years): try sample values to preview the calculator's result.
2. Embed width (px): 280 to 960 pixels (default 480), to fit your page column.
3. Copy the embed code shown in the results and paste it into your page's HTML.
The preview shows:
Estimated monthly payment (principal and interest)
Total of payments over the term
Embed code: an <iframe> tag
The code looks like this:
<iframe src="https://figsumo.com/mortgage-calculator" width="480" height="520" style="border:0;border-radius:8px" loading="lazy" title="Mortgage calculator"></iframe>
What your visitors get
The embedded calculator is the same tool as our main mortgage calculator: visitors enter their own loan amount, rate and term and get the monthly payment. It uses the standard fixed-rate formula:
M = P × r(1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)
where P is the loan amount, r the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the number of monthly payments.
Example
$320,000 at 6.5% for 30 years: $2,022.62 a month, with total payments of $728,142.36 (of which $408,142.36 is interest). A $250,000 loan at 6.0% over 15 years costs $2,109.64 a month.
Adding it to common platforms
WordPress (block editor): add a "Custom HTML" block and paste the code.
Squarespace: use a "Code" block.
Wix: use "Embed code" → "Embed HTML".
Shopify: edit a page in HTML view or use a custom liquid section.
Plain HTML: paste the code where you want the calculator to appear.
Some site builders strip iframes in certain plans or blocks; check your platform's help pages if the calculator doesn't appear.
Sizing tips
Use a width that matches your content column. 480 px suits most sidebars and narrow columns; 640–720 px works in a main column.
On mobile, the iframe keeps the width you set. If your theme has narrow mobile layouts, choose 320–360 px, or edit width to 100% and add max-width:480px to the style.
The loading="lazy" attribute means the calculator only loads when a visitor scrolls near it, so it won't slow your page's first load.
Important notes for site owners
The figures are estimates. The calculator shows principal and interest only. Taxes, insurance, PMI and HOA fees vary by property; mention this near the widget if your visitors are likely to be first-time buyers.
Lending regulations: if you are a lender or broker, advertising rules (such as Regulation Z in the US, FCA rules in the UK and provincial rules in Canada) may apply to how you present rates and payments. The calculator itself doesn't advertise a rate; the visitor enters their own.
Accessibility: keep the title attribute, which screen readers use to describe the frame.
Privacy: the calculator runs in the visitor's browser; check FigSumo's privacy policy for how the embedded page handles data.
Frequently asked questions
Is the mortgage calculator widget free?
Yes. There's no sign-up or fee.
Can I change the colours?
Not at present; the widget uses FigSumo's styling.
Does it work for Canadian and UK mortgages?
It uses monthly compounding at the entered rate, which suits US-style quotes. Canadian fixed-rate mortgages compound semi-annually, so link Canadian visitors to the Canada mortgage calculator and UK visitors to the UK mortgage calculator.
Will it slow down my site?
It loads lazily inside a frame, so its impact on your page speed is small.
