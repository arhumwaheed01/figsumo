H1: Day Counter
How many days until a holiday, a wedding or a deadline? How many days since a start date? This counter gives the number of days between any two dates, the equivalent in weeks, and whether the second date is before or after the first.
How to use this counter
1. From date: default 1 January 2026.
2. To date: default 31 December 2026.
Results: days between, weeks (approximate, to two decimals) and direction (forward if the "to" date is later, backward if earlier, with the number of days shown as positive).
How it's counted
Days between = the "to" date minus the "from" date. The start date isn't counted, but the end date is, which is the same as counting nights. From Monday to Tuesday is 1 day.
Including both ends: if you need to count both the first and last day (for example, a hotel's days of stay or a 7-day course from Monday to Sunday), add 1 to the result.
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
1 January to 31 December 2026 = 364 days, 52 weeks. Including both ends gives 365, the whole year.
Example 2: countdown to Christmas
15 October to 25 December 2026 = 71 days (10.14 weeks).
Example 3: days since
4 July 2026 to 15 October 2026 = 103 days.
Example 4: a leap year
1 January 2028 to 1 January 2029 = 366 days, because 2028 includes 29 February.
Example 5: counting backwards
Put the later date first and the counter shows the same number of days with the direction "Backward".
Where day counts matter
Notice periods and cooling-off periods: often counted in calendar days.
Interest: many loans and savings accounts accrue daily; the number of days determines the interest. US Treasury bills, UK gilts and Canadian money markets each use specific day-count conventions (actual/360, actual/365) [VERIFY for your product].
Residency and visa rules: the UK Statutory Residence Test, US Substantial Presence Test and Canadian 183-day rule all count days present, with specific rules for partial days.
Pregnancy, medication courses and training plans.

Understanding your results
Weeks are decimal: 10.14 weeks means 10 weeks and 1 day (0.14 × 7).
Calendar days, including weekends and holidays. Working days are usually about 5/7 of the total, minus public holidays.
Time of day is ignored.
Tips and common mistakes
Forgetting to add 1 when both the first and last days count.
Using calendar days for working-day deadlines.
Typing the wrong year, which the backward direction will flag.
Notes for US, Canada and UK users
US forms write dates month/day/year, while UK forms use day/month/year. Canadian forms use both, and the federal government recommends the ISO format, year-month-day. Before you count, check that 03/04/2026 means the date you intend: 4 March in the US, 3 April in the UK.
Frequently asked questions
How many days are between two dates?
Enter both dates; the result counts from the first to the second.
Does it include the start and end date?
It includes the end date but not the start. Add 1 to include both.
How many days are in a year?
365, or 366 in a leap year.
Can I count working days?
Not yet. Subtract weekends (about 2 in every 7 days) and public holidays.
What does "Backward" mean?
The "to" date is earlier than the "from" date.
How many days until 1 June 2027 from 15 October 2026?
229.
