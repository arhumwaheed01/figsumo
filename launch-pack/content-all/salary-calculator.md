H1: Salary Calculator
This salary calculator converts pay between hourly, weekly, monthly and annual figures. Enter what you're paid and how often, plus your weekly hours, and it shows the equivalent gross pay for each period. It's useful for comparing a job offer quoted as a salary with one quoted by the hour, or for budgeting from a monthly figure.
How to use this calculator
1. Pay amount: the figure you're paid, before tax.
2. Pay is per: Hour, Week, Month or Year.
3. Hours per week: your contracted or usual weekly hours. This is used to convert to and from hourly pay.
Results: estimated annual salary, monthly (gross), weekly (gross) and hourly (gross).
The formulas
The tool first converts your pay to an annual figure, then divides it back out:
Hourly → Annual = Rate × Hours per week × 52
Weekly → Annual = Weekly pay × 52
Monthly → Annual = Monthly pay × 12
Monthly = Annual ÷ 12
Weekly  = Annual ÷ 52
Hourly  = Annual ÷ (52 × Hours per week)
It assumes 52 paid weeks a year, so paid holidays count as working weeks. That's the standard way employers quote salaries.
Worked examples
Example 1: $30 an hour, 40 hours a week
Annual = 30 × 40 × 52 = $62,400.00
Monthly: $5,200.00; weekly: $1,200.00
If you're paid every two weeks (26 pay periods): $2,400.00 per paycheck; twice a month (24 pay periods): $2,600.00 per paycheck.
Example 2: a $75,000 salary
Hourly at 40 hours a week: $36.06
Monthly: $6,250.00; bi-weekly: $2,884.62
Adjusting for unpaid time. If you take 10 days off unpaid plus 11 public holidays (8 hours each), you'd work 1,912 hours a year. That makes your effective rate $39.23 an hour. This matters when you compare a salaried job with contract work that pays only for hours worked; see the freelance rate calculator.
Example 3: overtime
At $25 an hour for 40 hours, plus 5 hours of overtime at time-and-a-half in 48 weeks of the year, gross annual pay is $61,000.00. Enter overtime separately because the tool's hourly conversion assumes a single rate.
Example 4: a UK salary of £35,000 at 37.5 hours
Hourly: 35,000 ÷ (52 × 37.5) = £17.95
Monthly: £2,916.67; weekly: £673.08
Example 5: $24 an hour in Canada at 37.5 hours
Annual: 24 × 37.5 × 52 = $46,800.00
Monthly: $3,900.00; bi-weekly: $1,800.00

Understanding your results
All figures are gross. Tax, social insurance and pension contributions come out before you're paid. For take-home pay, use the US paycheck calculator, the Canada take-home pay calculator or the UK take-home pay calculator.
Months aren't equal. "Monthly" is annual ÷ 12. Someone paid weekly gets 52 paychecks a year, so some months have five paydays.
Bi-weekly isn't twice monthly. 26 bi-weekly payments is more than 24 semi-monthly payments; each check is smaller.
Part-time hours: 25 hours a week at $22 an hour is $28,600.00 a year.
Country notes
United States: the Fair Labor Standards Act requires overtime at 1.5 times the regular rate for non-exempt employees after 40 hours in a workweek. The federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour, but many states and cities set higher rates.
Canada: overtime rules and minimum wages are set by provinces, with federal rules for federally regulated workplaces. The federal minimum wage is indexed each April [VERIFY current rate]. Ontario's standard overtime threshold is 44 hours a week.
United Kingdom: the National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over is £12.71 an hour from April 2026, or £24,784.50 a year at 37.5 hours a week. A full-time week is often quoted as 37.5 hours: £32,000 a year is £16.41 an hour, or £615.38 a week. Full-time workers are entitled to 5.6 weeks of paid holiday (28 days, which can include bank holidays).
Tips and common mistakes
Using 40 hours when your contract says 37.5 (or the reverse).
Comparing gross salary to net pay. Compare like with like.
Forgetting benefits. Health insurance, pension matches and paid leave add to the value of a salaried offer.
Using 4 weeks a month. A month is about 4.33 weeks on average (52 ÷ 12).
Comparing job offers
When comparing offers, convert both to the same period and add the value of benefits: employer retirement contributions, health cover, paid leave and bonuses. A $70,000 salary with a 5% retirement match is worth about $3,500 a year more than the same salary without one. Also compare commuting costs and expected hours, because a higher salary with longer hours can mean a lower hourly rate.
Frequently asked questions
How do I convert hourly pay to a salary?
Multiply the hourly rate by weekly hours and by 52.
How much is $20 an hour per year?
At 40 hours a week, $41,600 a year before tax.
How many working hours are in a year?
2,080 at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks; fewer if you subtract unpaid time off.
Is the result before or after tax?
Before tax (gross).
Why does my monthly pay differ from the calculator?
Your employer may pay bi-weekly or weekly, or deduct taxes and benefits. The calculator shows gross annual ÷ 12.
How do I compare a contract rate with a salary?
Convert the salary to an hourly rate using the hours you'd actually work, then add the value of benefits and paid leave.
