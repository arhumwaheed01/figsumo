H1: Grade Calculator
How does a final exam change your course grade? Enter your current grade and how much of the course it covers, then your exam score and the exam's weight, and this calculator returns your weighted overall grade.
How to use this calculator
1. Current grade (%): your average so far (default 88).
2. Current weight (%): the share of the course that grade represents (default 70).
3. Final exam score (%): your actual or expected exam result (default 92).
4. Final exam weight (%): the exam's share of the course (default 30).
Results: weighted course grade and the weight total used. If the weights don't add up to 100, the calculator divides by their total, so 60% + 20% is treated as the 80% of the course completed so far.
The formula
course grade = (current × current weight + exam × exam weight) ÷ (current weight + exam weight)
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
(88 × 70 + 92 × 30) ÷ 100 = 89.2%
Example 2: what do you need on the final?
To finish with 90% when you have 88% on 70% of the course and the exam is worth 30%:
needed = (target − current × current weight) ÷ exam weight
       = (90 − 88 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30 = 94.67%
For a 93%, you'd need 104.67%. Above 100% means the target isn't reachable on the exam alone. (A dedicated mode for this is planned.)
Example 3: the worst case
Score 0 on the final and you'd finish with 88 × 0.70 = 61.6%. Your grade can't fall below this.
Example 4: several weighted parts
Homework 85% (20% of the grade), midterm 78% (30%), final 92% (50%): 85 × 0.2 + 78 × 0.3 + 92 × 0.5 = 86.4%.
Example 5: points-based classes
If your course uses points rather than weights, divide total points earned by total points possible. Two categories with weights 25 and 15 and scores of 90% and 80% give (90 × 25 + 80 × 15) ÷ 40 = 86.25%; enter them as "current" and "exam" to see the same result.

Understanding your results
Check the syllabus for exact weights, dropped lowest scores, extra credit and rounding policy.
Rounding: some teachers round 89.5 up to 90; others don't.
Minimum exam requirements: some courses require passing the final regardless of the overall grade.
Tips and common mistakes
Averaging the current grade and the exam equally when the weights differ.
Entering points instead of percentages.
Forgetting categories still to come (labs, projects).
Ignoring dropped scores in your current average.
Quick check
Scoring 45 out of 60 is 75%.
Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate my final grade?
Multiply each part by its weight and add, dividing by the total weight.
What do I need on my final to get an A?
(Target − current × current weight) ÷ exam weight, using decimals for weights.
What if my weights don't add to 100%?
The calculator divides by the total you enter.
Can I get above 100%?
Only with extra credit.
How much is my final worth?
It's in your syllabus; 20–40% is common.
Is a weighted grade the same as GPA?
No. A weighted grade is for one course; GPA averages courses by credits.
