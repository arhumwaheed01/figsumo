H1: Date Calculator
What date is 30 days from now? When does a 90-day notice period end? This calculator adds or subtracts a number of days from any start date and tells you the resulting date and the day of the week it falls on.
How to use this calculator
1. Start date: default 1 January 2026.
2. Days: how many days to move (a whole number of 0 or more).
3. Operation: "Add days" or "Subtract days".
Results: the result date (YYYY-MM-DD) and its day of the week.
How it works
The calculator moves forward or back one calendar day at a time, so month lengths (28–31 days) and leap years are handled automatically. The start date itself counts as day 0: adding 1 day to a Monday gives Tuesday.
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
1 January 2026 + 30 days = 31 January 2026, a Saturday.
Example 2: a 90-day deadline
15 October 2026 + 90 days = 2027-01-13, a Wednesday. A return window, probation period or visa allowance is often counted this way; check whether your contract counts the first day.
Example 3: counting back
A due date of 15 October 2026 minus 280 days (40 weeks) = 2026-01-08, the date pregnancy is conventionally counted from. For pregnancy dates, use the due date calculator.
Example 4: crossing February
2 March 2026 + 30 days = 2026-04-01, because March has 31 days.
Example 5: a milestone
1,000 days after 15 October 2026 is 2029-07-11 (Wednesday).
Calendar facts that affect date maths
Leap years: every year divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400. 2000 was a leap year; 2100 won't be.
Weeks: adding a multiple of 7 days always lands on the same weekday.
Months aren't fixed lengths, so "1 month" from 31 January has no single answer; that's why this tool counts days.
ISO 8601 format (YYYY-MM-DD) avoids the US (MM/DD) vs UK and Canadian (DD/MM) confusion: 03/04 is 4 March in the US and 3 April in the UK.
Business days and legal deadlines
The calculator counts calendar days, including weekends and holidays. Many deadlines use business days instead:
US federal holidays, Canadian statutory holidays and UK bank holidays differ, and some vary by state, province or nation (Scotland has different bank holidays from England).
Courts and tax authorities have their own rules for deadlines that fall on a weekend; the IRS, for example, moves a due date to the next business day.
Check the rule that applies before relying on a date for anything legal or financial.
Understanding your results
Day 0 is the start date. Some contracts count the start as day 1; subtract one from the days if so.
For the number of days between two dates, use the day counter.
Time zones don't change calendar arithmetic, but a deadline at midnight in one zone may already have passed in another; see the time zone calculator.
Tips and common mistakes
Off-by-one errors: decide whether the first or last day counts.
Mixing up date formats when typing.
Assuming 30 days = 1 month.
Forgetting public holidays for working-day deadlines.
