H1: Square Footage Calculator
Square footage is the area of a floor, room, lawn or wall in square feet. You need it for flooring, paint, carpet, rent comparisons, heating and cooling, and property listings. Enter the length and width of a rectangular space and this calculator gives the area in square feet and square metres.
How to use this calculator
1. Length (ft): default 15.
2. Width (ft): default 12.
Results: area in ft² and area (metric) in m².
Measurements in feet and inches? Convert inches to decimal feet first by dividing by 12 (6 in = 0.5 ft).
The formulas
area (ft²) = length × width
area (m²) = ft² × 0.092903
Worked examples
Example 1: the default room
15 × 12 = 180 ft² = 16.72 m², or 20 yd² (divide by 9) for carpet quoted per square yard.
Example 2: feet and inches
A room 12 ft 6 in × 10 ft 3 in: 12.50 × 10.25 = 128.12 ft².
Example 3: an L-shaped room
Split it into rectangles: 15 × 12 (180 ft²) plus an alcove 8 × 6 (48 ft²) = 228 ft². Calculate each rectangle with the calculator and add them.
Example 4: a metric room
A UK or Canadian room measured as 4.5 m × 3.6 m is 16.2 m² = 174.4 ft².
Example 5: a circular patio
Not a rectangle, so use πr²: a 10 ft diameter circle is π × 5² = 78.54 ft². The area calculator handles circles, triangles and more.
Example 6: flooring cost
180 ft² of flooring at $4.25/ft² with 10% waste: 180 × 1.10 × 4.25 = $841.50.
Measuring a room
1. Measure wall to wall at floor level, along the longest points.
2. Round to the nearest inch (or centimetre).
3. For irregular rooms, sketch the plan and split it into rectangles and triangles.
4. Subtract fixed features you won't cover (kitchen islands, fireplaces) only when ordering materials.
Square footage of a home
"Living area" in a property listing usually follows a standard:
US: ANSI Z765 counts finished, heated space above grade, measured to the outside of exterior walls; basements are reported separately. Practices vary by state and agent [VERIFY local MLS rules].
Canada: listings often follow provincial or RECA (Alberta) measurement standards.
UK: listings usually give gross internal area in m² and ft², following RICS guidance.
So a room-by-room total from this calculator will usually be smaller than the listed square footage.

Understanding your results
Waste allowance: add 5–15% when ordering flooring or tile; see the tile calculator.
Walls: for paint, multiply the perimeter by the wall height and subtract windows and doors.
Precision: results show two decimals.
Tips and common mistakes
Entering 12.6 for 12 ft 6 in (that's 12 ft 7.2 in).
Measuring at skirting-board height in rooms with uneven walls; take the longest dimension.
Forgetting closets and alcoves.
Confusing square feet with linear feet for trim and baseboards.
Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate square footage?
Multiply length by width in feet.
How do I calculate square footage of an L-shaped room?
Split it into rectangles and add their areas.
How many square feet is a 12 × 12 room?
144 ft².
How do I convert square feet to square metres?
Multiply by 0.092903.
How many square feet in an acre?
43,560.
Can I enter inches?
Convert to decimal feet (inches ÷ 12) first.
