H1: Mileage Reimbursement Calculator
If you drive your own car for work, your employer may reimburse you per mile or kilometre, and if you're self-employed you may be able to deduct a per-mile amount. This calculator works out the distance from your odometer readings and the reimbursement at the official 2026 rates for the US, Canada and the UK.
How to use this calculator
1. Starting odometer and ending odometer: or enter the distance directly (default 10,250 → 10,580 = 330 miles).
2. Country and purpose: US business, medical, or charity; Canada; or UK car, motorcycle or bicycle.
3. Distance already claimed this year: needed for the Canadian and UK tiered rates.
Results: distance driven and reimbursement.

The formula
distance = ending odometer − starting odometer
reimbursement = distance × rate   (split across tiers where they apply)
Worked examples
Example 1: US business trip
330 business miles in August 2026 × 76¢ = $250.80. The same trip in March would be $239.25.
Example 2: US medical and charity
120 miles to medical appointments after 1 July: $28.20 (deductible only as part of medical expenses above 7.5% of AGI if you itemise). 200 miles volunteering: $28.00.
Example 3: Canada
An employee drives 6,200 business km in 2026: 5,000 × $0.73 + 1,200 × $0.67 = $4,454.00.
Example 4: UK
12,000 business miles by car: 10,000 × 45p + 2,000 × 25p = £5,000.00. Carrying a colleague for 300 of those miles adds up to £15.00.
How the rates are used
US employees: reimbursements at or below the IRS rate under an accountable plan are tax-free. Unreimbursed employee mileage is no longer deductible for most employees (the suspension was made permanent).
US self-employed: you can deduct business miles at the standard rate instead of actual costs, but you must choose in the first year the car is used for business. It reduces income for self-employment tax too.
Canada: allowances at or below the CRA rates are generally tax-free to employees. Self-employed people deduct a business-use share of actual vehicle costs instead.
UK: payments up to AMAP are tax- and NIC-free. If your employer pays less, you can claim Mileage Allowance Relief on the difference.
Keep a mileage log
All three tax authorities expect records: date, destination, business purpose and distance for each trip. Commuting between home and a regular workplace is generally not business mileage.
Tips and common mistakes
Claiming commuting miles.
Using one US rate for the whole of 2026.
Forgetting the tier in Canada and the UK once you pass 5,000 km or 10,000 miles.
Using the rates as fuel cost. They cover fuel, wear, insurance and depreciation; for fuel only, use the fuel cost calculator.
Frequently asked questions
What is the IRS mileage rate for 2026?
72.5¢ per business mile for January to June and 76¢ from 1 July 2026.
What is the CRA mileage rate for 2026?
73¢/km for the first 5,000 km and 67¢/km after that, in the provinces.
What is the HMRC mileage rate?
45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles and 25p after.
Is mileage reimbursement taxable?
Not if it's at or below the official rate and properly documented.
Can I claim my commute?
Generally no.
Do I need a mileage log?
Yes; it's the main evidence for any claim.
