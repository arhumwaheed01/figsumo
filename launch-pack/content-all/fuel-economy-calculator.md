H1: Fuel Economy Calculator
Your car's trip computer and the official rating are estimates. The most reliable way to know your real fuel economy is to measure it from a fill-up. Enter the miles driven and the gallons used, and this calculator gives miles per gallon and the metric equivalent, litres per 100 kilometres.
How to measure your fuel economy
1. Fill the tank completely and note the odometer reading (or reset the trip meter).
2. Drive normally until you need fuel again.
3. Fill up completely again and note the gallons added (on the receipt) and the miles driven since the last fill.
4. Enter both numbers.
For the most accurate figure, average over several tanks.
How to use this calculator
1. Miles driven: default 300.
2. Gallons used: US gallons (default 10.5).
Results: fuel economy in mpg and litres per 100 km.
The formulas
mpg = miles ÷ gallons
L/100 km = (gallons × 3.785411784) ÷ (miles × 1.609344) × 100
The two measures are inverses: mpg is distance per unit of fuel (higher is better), while L/100 km is fuel per unit of distance (lower is better). A shortcut: L/100 km = 235.215 ÷ US mpg.
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
300 ÷ 10.5 = 28.57 mpg, or 8.23 L/100 km. In UK mpg that's 34.31, and in km per litre, 12.15.
Example 2: a highway tank
412 miles on 13.2 gallons = 31.21 mpg (7.54 L/100 km).
Example 3: Canada
A rating of 7.5 L/100 km equals about 31.4 US mpg.

Improving your mileage
Keep tyres inflated to the pressure on the door-jamb sticker.
Drive smoothly and keep to moderate highway speeds.
Remove roof boxes and extra weight when not needed.
Combine short trips; a cold engine uses more fuel.
Follow the maintenance schedule; use the recommended oil grade.
Understanding your results
Partial fills distort a single-tank figure. Always fill completely at both ends.
Pump shut-off varies, so averages over several tanks are more reliable.
Seasonal changes: winter fuel blends, cold starts and heaters can reduce mileage noticeably.
To turn mpg into trip costs, use the fuel cost calculator.
Tips and common mistakes
Using litres in the gallons box. Divide litres by 3.785 first.
Comparing UK mpg with US mpg directly.
Trusting the trip computer, which can read a few percent optimistic.
Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate mpg?
Miles driven ÷ gallons used.
How do I convert mpg to L/100 km?
235.215 ÷ mpg (US).
What is good fuel economy?
It depends on the vehicle; compare with its official rating and similar models.
Why is my mpg lower than advertised?
Ratings are lab tests; speed, weather, traffic and load reduce real-world figures.
Is UK mpg different from US mpg?
Yes, UK gallons are larger, so UK mpg is about 20% higher.
Can I enter kilometres and litres?
Not yet; convert to miles and gallons first.
