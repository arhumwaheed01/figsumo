H1: Day of the Week Calculator
Which day of the week were you born on? What day does a holiday fall on next year? Enter any date and this calculator tells you the weekday, along with the date in standard ISO format.
How to use this calculator
1. Date: pick or type a date (default 4 July 2026).
Results: the day of the week and the ISO date (YYYY-MM-DD).
How it works
The calculator uses the Gregorian calendar, which most of the world uses for civil dates, and counts days from a known reference to find the weekday. You can do the same by hand with Zeller's congruence:
h = (d + ⌊13(m + 1) ÷ 5⌋ + K + ⌊K ÷ 4⌋ + ⌊J ÷ 4⌋ + 5J) mod 7
d: day of the month
m: month (3 = March … 14 = February; January and February count as months 13 and 14 of the previous year)
K: year within the century (year mod 100)
J: century (⌊year ÷ 100⌋)
h: 0 = Saturday, 1 = Sunday, 2 = Monday … 6 = Friday
Worked examples
Example 1: 4 July 2026
d = 4, m = 7, K = 26, J = 20: 4 + ⌊104 ÷ 5⌋ (20) + 26 + 6 + 5 + 100 = 161; 161 mod 7 = 0 → Saturday. That's the 250th anniversary of the US Declaration of Independence.
Example 2: the Moon landing
20 July 1969 was a Sunday.
Example 3: the millennium
1 January 2000 was a Saturday.
Example 4: Christmas 2026
25 December 2026 is a Friday.
Shortcuts for mental calculation
Same date, next year: the weekday moves forward one day, or two if a 29 February falls in between. 15 October 2026 is a Thursday, so 15 October 2027 is a Friday.
Doomsday rule: certain easy dates share a weekday each year: 4/4, 6/6, 8/8, 10/10, 12/12, the last day of February, and 9/5 and 5/9 (and 7/11, 11/7). In 2026 they're all Saturdays.
The calendar repeats every 28 years within a century (when no century non-leap year intervenes); the full Gregorian cycle is 400 years.
Weekday names
The English names come from Old English and Norse: Sunday (Sun), Monday (Moon), Tuesday (Tiw), Wednesday (Woden), Thursday (Thor), Friday (Frigg) and Saturday (Saturn).
Week conventions
US and Canada: calendars usually start the week on Sunday.
UK, Europe and ISO 8601: the week starts on Monday, and week 1 is the week containing the year's first Thursday. 15 October 2026 is in ISO week 42 and is day 288 of the year.
Understanding your results
Historical dates: Britain and its colonies switched from the Julian to the Gregorian calendar in September 1752, skipping 11 days. For dates before a country's switch, a Gregorian weekday may not match contemporary records.
ISO format avoids MM/DD vs DD/MM confusion.
Tips and common mistakes
Typing DD/MM in a US-format field (or vice versa).
Forgetting the January/February adjustment in Zeller's formula.
Applying Gregorian rules to old Julian dates.
Practice
1 January 2000 was a Saturday; Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday.
