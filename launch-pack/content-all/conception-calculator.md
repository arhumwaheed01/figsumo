H1: Conception Date Calculator
Conception happens when sperm fertilises an egg, usually within a day of ovulation. Because ovulation dates vary and sperm can survive for several days, no calculator can pinpoint the exact day, but you can estimate it from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) or from your due date. This calculator works in both directions.
How to use this calculator
1. Known date: choose Last menstrual period or Due date.
2. Enter the LMP date or the due date.
3. Cycle length: your usual cycle, 21–45 days (default 28).
Results:
LMP mode: estimated conception date and estimated due date
Due-date mode: estimated conception date and estimated LMP
How it works
Conception ≈ LMP + (cycle length − 14) days
Due date   = LMP + 280 days
LMP (from due date) = due date − 280 days
The 14 days is the average luteal phase (from ovulation to the next period). For a 28-day cycle, conception is estimated on day 14.
In the current tool, the due date is always LMP + 280 days, whatever cycle length you enter, while the conception date moves with cycle length. For cycles much longer or shorter than 28 days, your clinician may adjust the due date, usually after a dating scan.
Worked examples
Example 1: LMP 4 May 2026, 28-day cycle
Estimated conception: 18 May 2026
Due date: 8 February 2027
Most likely window for the sex that led to conception: about 13 May 2026 to 19 May 2026 (sperm survive up to five days)
Example 2: due date 14 February 2027, 28-day cycle
Estimated LMP: 10 May 2026
Estimated conception: 24 May 2026
Example 3: LMP 4 May 2026, 32-day cycle
Conception: 22 May 2026, four days later than Example 1.
Many clinicians would also move the due date four days later, to 12 February 2027; the tool doesn't yet.
Understanding your results
It's an estimate. Even with regular cycles, ovulation can shift by several days.
Conception isn't necessarily the date of sex. Sperm can live up to five days, so conception can happen days after intercourse.
Dating scans are more accurate. A first-trimester ultrasound is the best way to date a pregnancy; it can move the estimated conception date too.
IVF: conception is known precisely from the embryo transfer and embryo age, so this calculator isn't needed.
Paternity questions: a calendar estimate can't establish paternity reliably when possible dates are close together. Only DNA testing can.

When to take a pregnancy test
Home tests detect hCG, a hormone produced after the embryo implants, usually 6 to 12 days after ovulation. For the 28-day cycle in Example 1, the next period would be due on 1 June 2026, and a test from that day is far more reliable than an early test. A negative result before a missed period doesn't rule out pregnancy; test again a few days later if your period hasn't started.
Pregnancy age vs fetal age
Pregnancy is counted from LMP (gestational age). Fetal age (from conception) is about two weeks less. At the due date, gestational age is 40 weeks and fetal age about 38 weeks.
Country notes
Dating conventions are the same in the US, Canada and the UK: all count from LMP and confirm with ultrasound. In the UK, the NHS dating scan is at 10–14 weeks; in the US and Canada, first-trimester ultrasound is commonly offered.
Tips and common mistakes
Using the last day of the period. Use the first day of bleeding.
Assuming conception was on the date of sex.
Ignoring your scan date. If your care team gave you a due date from a scan, use due-date mode.
Planning a pregnancy: advice by country
Folic acid: the NHS, the US CDC and Health Canada all advise 400 micrograms (0.4 mg) of folic acid a day for anyone who could become pregnant, ideally starting before conception and continuing through early pregnancy. Some people are advised a higher dose by their doctor.
When to ask for help, US: ACOG suggests an evaluation if you haven't conceived after 12 months of trying when under 35, after 6 months at 35 or over, and sooner over 40.
When to ask for help, UK: NICE advises seeing a GP if you haven't conceived after a year of regular unprotected sex, or earlier if you're 36 or over or have a known fertility issue.
When to ask for help, Canada: Canadian guidance follows the same 12-month and 6-month (age 35+) pattern [VERIFY against current SOGC guidance].
Cycle tracking is a sensible first step, but it doesn't replace a check-up if you have very irregular cycles, painful periods or a known condition.
Frequently asked questions
How do I work out when I conceived?
Add your cycle length minus 14 days to the first day of your last period.
Can I find the conception date from my due date?
Yes: subtract 280 days to get your LMP, then add cycle length minus 14.
How accurate is a conception date calculator?
It's usually within a few days, but not exact.
Can I get pregnant from sex a week before ovulation?
Unlikely; sperm survive up to about five days.
Why is my pregnancy two weeks "older" than conception?
Pregnancy is dated from your last period, about two weeks before ovulation.
Does cycle length affect my due date?
It can; longer cycles often mean a later due date.
