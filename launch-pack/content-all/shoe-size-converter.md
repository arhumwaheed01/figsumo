H1: Shoe Size Converter
Shoe sizes differ between the US, UK and Europe, and between men's, women's and children's ranges, which makes buying shoes online across borders tricky. This converter maps US men's sizes to European (EU) sizes and back, and the tables below cover UK and women's sizes too.
How to use this converter
1. Convert: "US men's → EU" or "EU → US men's".
2. Shoe size: whole or half sizes (default 10).
Result: the approximate size in the other system.
Size systems at a glance
US: based on the length of the shoe's last in thirds of an inch, with separate men's and women's scales (women's is about 1.5 sizes larger for the same foot).
UK: also in thirds of an inch but starts from a different zero; UK men's is usually ½ to 1 size smaller than US men's. UK sizes are unisex.
EU (Paris points): steps of ⅔ cm along the last; one scale for everyone.
Mondopoint (JP/ski boots): the foot length in millimetres.

Worked examples
Example 1: buying European shoes
You wear US men's 10. A European brand lists EU 43 and 44: start with 43–44 and check the brand's chart for the foot length in centimetres.
Example 2: US women's to UK
US women's 8.5 is about UK 6 and EU 39–40. Women's US to UK is usually US − 2 (some brands use − 2.5).
Example 3: UK to US men's
A UK 9 is about US men's 9.5–10.
Measure your foot
The most reliable way to buy across systems is by length:
1. Stand on a sheet of paper against a wall, heel touching the wall.
2. Mark the tip of your longest toe; measure heel to mark in centimetres.
3. Measure both feet in the evening, when feet are largest, and use the longer one.
4. Compare with the brand's chart, which often lists foot length (or Mondopoint) for each size.
Allow about 1–1.5 cm of room beyond your toes for most shoes; running shoes often need a thumb's width.
Children's sizes
Children's US and UK sizes run on separate scales that restart after 13 (US 13C → youth 1). EU sizes continue on the same scale, so EU is often the simplest system for kids. Measure every few months; children's feet grow quickly.
Width
US widths run from narrow (A/B) through medium (D for men, B for women) to wide (E, EE, 4E). UK widths use letters such as E, F, G and H. EU sizing rarely specifies width; look for "wide fit" ranges.
Understanding your results
Every conversion is approximate. There's no single international standard everyone uses.
Half sizes don't always exist in EU sizing.
Fit varies by style: boots, trainers, heels and sandals of the same nominal size fit differently.
Tips and common mistakes
Confusing UK and US sizes; UK numbers are smaller.
Using men's conversions for women's shoes.
Measuring in the morning.
Ignoring width.
Frequently asked questions
What is a US men's 10 in EU?
About 43–44.
How do UK and US sizes compare?
UK is usually ½–1 size smaller than US men's and about 2 sizes smaller than US women's.
How do I convert women's to men's sizes?
Subtract about 1.5 from the US women's size.
What's the most accurate method?
Measure foot length in centimetres and use the brand's chart.
Are EU sizes unisex?
Yes.
