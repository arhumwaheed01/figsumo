H1: Unit Converter
Converting between metric and imperial units comes up constantly in North America and the UK: recipes in grams, road signs in miles, weather in Celsius or Fahrenheit. This converter handles the everyday conversions for length, weight and temperature using exact defined factors.
How to use this converter
1. Value: the number to convert (default 1).
2. Category: Length, Mass / weight, or Temperature.
3. From unit and To unit: choose two units from the same category.
Length: meters, kilometers, feet, inches, miles
Mass: kilograms, pounds
Temperature: °C, °F
Result: the converted value to six decimal places. Mixing categories (for example feet to pounds) shows an error.
How it works
Length and mass convert through a base unit: the value is multiplied by the "from" unit's size in meters (or kilograms) and divided by the "to" unit's size. Temperature uses formulas because the scales have different zero points.

Quick mental conversions
km to miles: multiply by 0.6 (a 5 km run ≈ 3.1 mi).
kg to lb: double and add 10% (70 kg → 140 + 14 = 154 lb).
°C to °F: double and add 30 (20 °C ≈ 70 °F; exact is 68).
Feet to metres: divide by 3.3.
US, Canada and UK usage
United States: customary units for almost everything outside science and medicine; Fahrenheit for weather.
Canada: metric officially, but height in feet and inches and body weight in pounds are common; cooking and construction often mix both.
United Kingdom: metric for most trade, but road distances and speeds in miles, pints for beer and milk, and body weight often in stones (1 st = 14 lb). See the weight converter.
US vs UK volumes differ: a US gallon is 3.785 L and a UK gallon 4.546 L.
Understanding your results
Six decimals are shown; round to suit the purpose (two for a recipe, more for engineering).
Pounds here are avoirdupois pounds, the everyday kind. Troy pounds for precious metals differ.
Mass vs weight: the kilogram and pound here are masses; on Earth they're used interchangeably for weight.
Tips and common mistakes
Picking units from different categories.
Converting temperature differences with the full formula. A change of 10 °C is a change of 18 °F (no +32).
Rounding too early in multi-step conversions.
Using 2.2 for pounds per kilogram in precise work (it's 2.20462…).
Kitchen check
A US cup is about 236.6 mL; the metric cup used in Canada is 250 mL.
Frequently asked questions
How many feet are in a meter?
3.28084.
How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
Multiply by 9/5 and add 32.
How many kilometers are in a mile?
1.609344, exactly.
How many pounds are in a kilogram?
About 2.20462.
Can I convert cups or liters?
Not yet; volume is a planned category.
Why is −40 the same in °C and °F?
It's where the two formulas give the same number.
