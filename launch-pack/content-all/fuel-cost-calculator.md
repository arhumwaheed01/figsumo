H1: Fuel Cost Calculator
Planning a road trip or comparing commuting costs? This calculator works out how much fuel a journey uses and what it costs, from the distance, your vehicle's fuel economy and the price of fuel.
How to use this calculator
1. Trip distance (mi): one way, or double it for a round trip (default 120).
2. Fuel economy (mpg): US miles per gallon (default 28). Use a real-world figure from your trip computer or fill-up records if you have it.
3. Price per gallon ($): default 3.75.
Results: trip fuel cost and gallons used.
The formula
gallons = distance ÷ mpg
cost = gallons × price per gallon
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
120 mi ÷ 28 mpg = 4.29 gallons × $3.75 = $16.07
Example 2: a weekend round trip, shared
450 miles each way in a car averaging 32 mpg, with fuel at $3.40: 900 ÷ 32 = 28.12 gallons = $95.62, or $31.88 each for three people.
Example 3: Canada (metric)
A 500 km trip in a car using 7.5 L/100 km needs 500 × 7.5 ÷ 100 = 37.5 L. At $1.55 a litre: $58.12. Until metric mode is added, convert with the fuel economy calculator.
Example 4: the UK
A 200-mile journey at 45 mpg (UK gallons) uses 20.2 litres; at £1.40 a litre that's £28.29. UK mpg figures use the 4.546 L imperial gallon, so don't enter them as US mpg without converting (UK mpg × 0.8327 = US mpg).
Example 5: yearly running cost
Driving 12,000 miles a year at $3.75: 28 mpg costs $1,607.14; 35 mpg costs $1,285.71, saving $321.43 a year.
What affects real-world fuel use
Speed: fuel use rises sharply above about 50 mph. The US DOE estimates each 5 mph over 50 is like paying an extra 30¢ or so per gallon [VERIFY current figure].
Aggressive driving: hard acceleration and braking can cut highway mileage by 15–30%.
Load and roof racks: extra weight and drag.
Tyre pressure: under-inflation increases rolling resistance.
Air conditioning and short trips: cold engines and AC use more fuel.
Idling burns fuel for zero miles.
Fuel prices in the US, Canada and UK
US: sold by the gallon; prices vary by state, largely because of state fuel taxes. Check AAA or the EIA for averages.
Canada: sold by the litre in cents per litre, with provincial taxes and carbon-pricing differences.
UK: sold by the litre in pence per litre, with fuel duty and VAT making up a large share.
Understanding your results
The result is fuel only. For the full cost of driving (wear, insurance, depreciation), the IRS standard mileage rate or HMRC approved mileage rates give a broader per-mile figure; see the mileage reimbursement calculator.
Rated vs real mpg: EPA and WLTP ratings are lab-based; most drivers see somewhat lower figures.
Electric vehicles: use the electricity calculator with kWh per mile.
Tips and common mistakes
Forgetting the return trip.
Using UK mpg as US mpg (UK figures look about 20% better).
Using the best-case rated mpg for a mountainous or high-speed trip.
Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate fuel cost for a trip?
Distance ÷ mpg × price per gallon.
How many gallons will I use?
Distance divided by mpg.
How do I split fuel costs?
Divide the total by the number of people.
Can I use litres and kilometres?
Not yet. Convert as in Example 3, or multiply L/100 km by distance ÷ 100.
Does this include tolls or parking?
No; add them separately.
What's the difference between US and UK mpg?
UK gallons are about 20% larger, so UK mpg figures are higher for the same car.
