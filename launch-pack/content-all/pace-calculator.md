H1: Pace Calculator
Pace is the time it takes to cover one kilometre or one mile. Runners use it to plan races and training; walkers and cyclists use speed more often. This calculator turns a distance and a finish time into pace per kilometre, pace per mile, and speed in km/h and mph.
How to use this calculator
1. Distance unit: kilometres or miles.
2. Distance: for example 5 for a 5K, or 26.2 miles.
3. Time: hours, minutes and seconds.
Results: pace in min/km, pace in min/mi, speed in km/h and speed in mph.
Formulas
Pace (min per km) = total time in minutes ÷ distance in km
Speed (km/h)      = distance in km ÷ time in hours
Pace per mile     = pace per km × 1.609344
A mile is exactly 1.609344 km. The decimal part of the pace is converted to seconds (× 60).
Worked examples
Example 1: 5 km in 28:00
Pace = 28 ÷ 5 = 5.6 minutes = 5:36 min/km
Per mile: 9:01 min/mi
Speed: 10.71 km/h (6.66 mph)
Example 2: marathon (42.195 km) in 3:30:00

Example 3: 3.1 miles in 25:10
Pace = 8:07 min/mi, or 5:03 min/km

Using pace in training
Easy runs are usually 1 to 2 minutes per km slower than 5K race pace; you should be able to hold a conversation.
Tempo runs are "comfortably hard", near the pace you could hold for an hour.
Intervals are run at or faster than 5K pace with recovery between.
Heart rate is another way to set effort; see the target heart rate calculator.
Understanding your results
GPS watches drift. Tree cover and tall buildings can add or remove distance, so pace on a watch may not match a measured course.
Hills and wind slow pace without meaning lower fitness.
Treadmill pace often feels easier than outdoor pace; a 1% incline is commonly used to compensate.
Country notes
The US and UK mostly use miles for road races (though 5K and 10K are metric distances everywhere), while Canada uses kilometres. Track events are metric worldwide.
Tips and common mistakes
Entering 1:30 as 1.30 hours. Use the separate hours and minutes fields.
Using the wrong unit. A 10-mile run entered as 10 km gives a pace 1.6 times too fast.
Planning an even pace on a hilly course. Aim for even effort instead.
Quick check
A 5 km parkrun in 27:30 is a pace of 5.5 min/km, or 5:30 per km.
A 10-mile run at 9:00 per mile takes 1 hour 30 minutes.
Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate my running pace?
Divide your time by distance.
What is a good 5K pace?
It depends on age and experience; many recreational runners finish in 25 to 35 minutes, or 5:00 to 7:00 per km.
How do I convert min/km to min/mile?
Multiply by 1.609344.
What pace do I need for a sub-4-hour marathon?
About 5:41 per km or 9:09 per mile.
Is pace or speed better?
Runners mostly use pace; cyclists use speed.
How do I convert pace to speed?
Speed in km/h = 60 ÷ pace in min/km.
