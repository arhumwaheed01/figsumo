H1: Sleep Calculator
Waking up at the end of a sleep cycle, rather than in the middle of deep sleep, can leave you feeling less groggy. This calculator works backwards from the time you need to get up and suggests when to go to bed, based on 90-minute sleep cycles plus time to fall asleep.
How to use this calculator
1. Wake-up time (HH:MM): in 24-hour format (default 07:00).
2. Sleep cycles (90 min each): 3 to 6 (default 5).
Results: suggested bedtime and time in bed (hours).
How it works
time in bed = cycles × 90 minutes + 15 minutes
bedtime = wake-up time − time in bed
The 15 minutes is a typical time to fall asleep.
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
Wake at 07:00 with 5 cycles: 5 × 90 + 15 = 465 minutes (7.75 h, shown as 7.8) → bed at 23:15.

What happens in a sleep cycle
Each night you pass through roughly four to six cycles, each lasting about 80–110 minutes:
1. N1 (light sleep): a few minutes of drifting off.
2. N2: body temperature drops, heart rate slows; most of the night is spent here.
3. N3 (deep, slow-wave sleep): hardest to wake from; important for physical recovery. More of it happens early in the night.
4. REM sleep: vivid dreams and memory processing; REM periods lengthen towards morning.
Ninety minutes is an average. Your cycles vary from night to night and through the night, so treat the suggested bedtime as a guide, not a precise target.

Tips for better sleep
Keep a consistent schedule, including weekends.
Get morning daylight and limit bright screens in the hour before bed.
Avoid caffeine late in the day (its effects can last 6 hours or more) and heavy meals or alcohol close to bedtime.
Keep the bedroom cool, dark and quiet.
If you can't sleep after about 20 minutes, get up and do something calm until you feel sleepy.
When to get help
Persistent trouble sleeping, loud snoring with gasping, or severe daytime sleepiness can signal conditions such as insomnia or sleep apnoea. Talk to a doctor (a GP in the UK).
Understanding your results
Times are 24-hour: 23:15 is 11:15 pm.
Falling asleep takes longer for some people; move the bedtime earlier if you usually need 30 minutes.
Naps: a 20-minute nap avoids deep sleep; a 90-minute nap covers a full cycle.
Frequently asked questions
What time should I go to bed to wake up at 7 am?
Around 23:15 for five cycles, or 21:45 for six.
How long is a sleep cycle?
About 90 minutes on average (80–110).
Is it better to sleep 6 or 7.5 hours?
7.5 hours meets adult recommendations; 6 doesn't.
Why add 15 minutes?
It's a typical time to fall asleep.
Can I enter 12-hour times?
Use 24-hour format (07:00, 23:30).
Does this work for shift workers?
Yes; enter your actual wake-up time.
