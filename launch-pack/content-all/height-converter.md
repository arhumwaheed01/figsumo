H1: Height Converter
Height is given in feet and inches in the US and, informally, in Canada and the UK, but in centimetres on passports, medical records and most of the world's forms. This converter switches between the two.
How to use this converter
1. Convert: "Centimeters → feet & inches" or "Feet & inches → centimeters".
2. Enter height (cm), or feet and inches.
Results:
cm → ft & in: feet and inches (to 0.1 in) and total inches.
ft & in → cm: centimetres (to 0.1 cm).
The formulas
1 inch = 2.54 cm (exact)
total inches = cm ÷ 2.54
feet = whole part of (total inches ÷ 12)
inches = total inches − 12 × feet
cm = (feet × 12 + inches) × 2.54
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
175 cm ÷ 2.54 = 68.90 in → 5 ft 8.9 in
Example 2: feet and inches to centimetres
5 ft 9 in = 69 in × 2.54 = 175.3 cm. 6 ft = 182.9 cm.
Example 3: 160 cm
160 ÷ 2.54 = 62.99 in → 5 ft 3.0 in (almost exactly 5 ft 3 in).
Example 4: 5 ft 4 in
64 in × 2.54 = 162.6 cm.

Average heights
Average adult heights differ by country and over time. Typical recent figures are about 5′9″ (175 cm) for men and 5′3½″ (161 cm) for women in the US, and around 5′9½″ (176–178 cm) and 5′4″ (163–164 cm) in Canada and the UK [VERIFY latest national surveys].
Predicting a child's adult height
A common rough method is the mid-parental height:
boy: (mother's height + father's height + 13 cm) ÷ 2
girl: (mother's height + father's height − 13 cm) ÷ 2
With a father of 178 cm and a mother of 165 cm: a boy's target is about 178.0 cm and a girl's 165.0 cm, with a typical range of about ±8.5 cm. Nutrition, health and genetics all play a part; growth charts from a paediatrician are more informative. (This calculator doesn't yet include the prediction.)
Measuring height accurately
Stand barefoot on a hard floor with heels, buttocks and shoulders against a wall.
Look straight ahead; place a flat object (a book) on your head at a right angle to the wall.
Mark the wall and measure from the floor.
You're slightly taller in the morning; the spine compresses during the day by up to about 1 cm.
Understanding your results
Rounding: 5 ft 8.9 in is often rounded to 5′9″ in everyday use.
Height for BMI: the BMI calculator accepts either unit system.
Forms: passports and driving licences may require whole centimetres or inches.
Tips and common mistakes
Typing 5.9 to mean 5 ft 9 in. 5.9 ft is 5 ft 10.8 in. Use the separate feet and inches boxes.
Using 2.5 cm per inch (it's 2.54).
Measuring with shoes on.
Notes for US, Canada and UK users
US driver's licences print height in feet and inches, as many Canadian provincial licences do alongside or instead of centimetres. UK passports and driving licences don't show height at all, but NHS records and clinic forms use centimetres. In all three countries, children's growth charts plot height in centimetres, with inches as a secondary scale on some US charts.
