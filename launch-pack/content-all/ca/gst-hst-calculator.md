H1: GST/HST Calculator
In Canada, sales tax on most goods and services is the federal Goods and Services Tax (GST) or, in five provinces, the Harmonized Sales Tax (HST), which combines GST and a provincial portion. Some provinces add their own provincial sales tax instead. This calculator adds the right taxes for your province to a pre-tax price, or works backwards from a total to the pre-tax amount.
How to use this calculator
1. Province or territory: where the supply is made (for goods, usually where they're delivered).
2. Amount: the price.
3. Amount is: before tax or after tax.
Results: GST or HST, PST/QST where it applies, the total, or (reverse mode) the pre-tax amount and tax included.

Provincial sales taxes (PST, RST, QST) are administered by the provinces and can apply to a different list of goods and services than GST/HST.
The formulas
Tax = Price × Rate
Total = Price × (1 + Total rate)
Pre-tax price = Total ÷ (1 + Total rate)
In Quebec, BC, Manitoba and Saskatchewan, the provincial tax is calculated on the price before GST, so the rates simply add up.
Worked examples
Example 1: $100 in Ontario
HST = 100 × 13% = $13.00; total $113.00
Example 2: $100 in Quebec
GST = $5.00; QST = 100 × 9.975% = $9.98
Total = $114.98
In British Columbia, the same purchase is $112.00 (5% GST + 7% PST).
Example 3: removing HST from a receipt
You paid $56.50 in Ontario.
Pre-tax price = 56.50 ÷ 1.13 = $50.00; HST included = $6.50
Example 4: Nova Scotia's rate cut
A $250 purchase in Nova Scotia now carries $35.00 of HST at 14%, compared with $37.50 at the previous 15% rate.
Example 5: removing QST and GST from a Quebec receipt
You paid $57.49 in Quebec.
Pre-tax price = 57.49 ÷ 1.14975 = $50.00
GST $2.50 and QST $4.99
Example 6: a $1,200 laptop shipped to different provinces
The rate depends on where it's delivered: $1,260.00 in Alberta, $1,356.00 in Ontario and $1,380.00 in New Brunswick. That's a $120.00 difference for the same item.

For businesses
Registration: you must register for GST/HST once your worldwide taxable supplies exceed $30,000.00 over four consecutive calendar quarters (the small supplier threshold). You can register voluntarily below it.
Input tax credits: registrants recover the GST/HST they pay on business purchases. If you charge $455.00 of HST on $3,500 of sales and paid $260.00 on $2,000 of business purchases, you remit the difference: $195.00.
Zero-rated and exempt supplies: basic groceries, prescription drugs and medical devices are zero-rated (taxed at 0%, input tax credits allowed). Many health, educational and financial services and long-term residential rents are exempt (no tax, no input tax credits).
Place of supply rules decide which province's rate applies when selling across provinces.
Understanding your results
Rounding: businesses round tax to the cent, so a cent of difference is normal.
Rebates: some provinces rebate their portion of HST on items such as children's clothing, books or diapers (Ontario point-of-sale rebates, for example), so the tax at the till may be lower than the full rate.
Households: the GST/HST credit is a tax-free quarterly payment to lower-income households, based on your tax return.
Tips and common mistakes
Using the wrong province. Online purchases are taxed at the rate where they're delivered.
Taking 13% of a total to find the HST. Divide by 1.13 instead.
Forgetting PST in BC, Manitoba, Saskatchewan or Quebec.
Charging tax on zero-rated basic groceries.
Notes for US and UK readers
Canadian shelf prices usually exclude GST/HST and PST, like US prices exclude state sales tax, so the total at the till is higher than the sticker. That's the opposite of the UK, where VAT is included in displayed prices. Visitors to Canada generally can't reclaim GST/HST on goods they take home, as the federal visitor rebate programme ended in 2007, although some tour packages qualify for a rebate [VERIFY]. For US state sales tax, see the sales tax calculator; for UK VAT, the VAT calculator.

Frequently asked questions
What is the difference between GST and HST?
GST is the 5% federal tax. HST combines GST with a provincial portion in Ontario, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island.
What is the sales tax in Ontario?
13% HST.
What is the sales tax in Quebec?
5% GST plus 9.975% QST, 14.975% in total.
How do I calculate GST backwards?
Divide the total by 1 plus the rate (1.05 for 5% GST), then subtract.
When do I need to register for GST/HST?
Once your taxable supplies exceed $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters.
Is there GST on groceries?
Basic groceries are zero-rated; snack foods, soft drinks and restaurant meals are taxable.
