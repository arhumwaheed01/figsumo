H1: Time Duration Calculator
How long is it from 08:15 to 12:45? From 21:30 to 06:10 the next morning? This calculator gives the duration between two clock times in hours and minutes, and in total minutes.
How to use this calculator
1. Start (HH:MM): 24-hour format (default 08:15).
2. End (HH:MM): default 12:45.
Results: duration in hours and minutes and total minutes.
If the end time is earlier than the start, the calculator assumes the period runs past midnight into the next day.
How it works
duration = end − start            (in minutes)
if negative, add 1,440 (24 hours)
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
08:15 to 12:45 = 4 h 30 min (270 minutes).
Example 2: overnight
21:30 to 06:10 = 8 h 40 min (520 minutes).
Example 3: a short meeting
13:05 to 13:55 = 50 min.
Example 4: a flight (same time zone)
Departing 05:50 and landing 07:05 local time in the same zone = 1 h 15 min. When a flight crosses time zones, convert both times to the same zone first with the time zone calculator.
Counting by hand
1. Count up from the start to the next full hour.
2. Count whole hours to the last full hour before the end.
3. Add the remaining minutes.
08:15 → 09:00 is 45 min; 09:00 → 12:00 is 3 h; 12:00 → 12:45 is 45 min. Total: 3 h 90 min = 4 h 30 min.

Daylight saving time
The calculator doesn't know about clock changes. If the period spans the night the clocks go forward or back, adjust by an hour:
US and Canada: clocks go forward on the second Sunday in March and back on the first Sunday in November (most provinces and states; Saskatchewan, Arizona and Hawaii don't observe it).
UK: forward on the last Sunday in March, back on the last Sunday in October.
A night shift from 22:00 to 06:00 is 7 hours, not 8, on the night clocks go forward.
Where durations matter
Payroll: with breaks deducted, see the time card calculator.
Billing: in 6- or 15-minute increments.
Sleep: time in bed; see the sleep calculator.
Parking and childcare: charged by the hour or part hour.
Understanding your results
Maximum duration is 23 h 59 min, since only clock times (not dates) are entered. For longer periods, use the day counter.
Equal start and end times give 0, not 24 hours.
Decimal hours: divide total minutes by 60 (270 ÷ 60 = 4.5).
Tips and common mistakes
Entering 12-hour times without converting. 1:00 means 01:00 (1 am).
Forgetting DST changes on overnight periods.
Mixing time zones for travel.
Practice
08:45 to 17:20 is 8 hours 35 minutes.
Frequently asked questions
How do I calculate the time between two times?
Subtract the start from the end; add 24 hours if the end is the next day.
How long is it from 9 am to 5 pm?
8 hours.
Can it handle times past midnight?
Yes; an earlier end time is treated as the next day.
Does it account for daylight saving?
No; adjust by an hour on clock-change nights.
Can I include dates?
Not yet. Use the day counter for multi-day periods.
How do I get decimal hours?
Divide total minutes by 60.
