H1: Time Calculator
Adding up durations in your head goes wrong as soon as minutes pass 60. This calculator adds or subtracts two lengths of time given in hours and minutes and returns the result in hours and minutes, plus the total in minutes.
How to use this calculator
1. First time: hours and minutes (default 2 h 45 min).
2. Second time: hours and minutes (default 1 h 30 min).
3. Operation: Add or Subtract.
Results: total time in hours and minutes and total minutes. If you subtract a longer time from a shorter one, the result is shown as negative.
How it works
total minutes = (h₁ × 60 + m₁) ± (h₂ × 60 + m₂)
hours = total minutes ÷ 60 (whole part)
minutes = total minutes mod 60
Working in minutes avoids the carry-over errors that happen with base-60 arithmetic.
Worked examples
Example 1: the default
2 h 45 min + 1 h 30 min = 165 + 90 = 255 minutes = 4 h 15 min.
Example 2: subtracting
1 h 30 min − 2 h 45 min = −1 h 15 min (−75 minutes).
Example 3: a playlist or project
Adding several durations (1 h 12 min, 58 min, 2 h 5 min and 47 min) two at a time gives 5 h 2 min (302 minutes). Add the first two, then add the next to the result, and so on.
Example 4: time remaining
An 8-hour shift with 5 h 50 min already worked leaves 2 h 10 min.
Example 5: doubling a recipe time
Some processes scale with quantity: 3 h 45 min twice over is 3 h 45 + 3 h 45 = 7 h 30 min. (Most cooking times don't double when you double the quantity.)
Base-60 arithmetic by hand
Add the minutes and the hours separately, then carry:
  2 h 45 min
+ 1 h 30 min
= 3 h 75 min → 75 min = 1 h 15 min → 4 h 15 min
To subtract, borrow 60 minutes from the hours when needed:
  5 h 10 min − 2 h 40 min → 4 h 70 min − 2 h 40 min = 2 h 30 min

Where time arithmetic comes up
Timesheets and billing: totalling hours across a week.
Travel: adding legs and layovers (check time zones with the time zone calculator).
Video and audio editing: summing clip lengths.
Fitness: total training time across sessions.
Cooking and baking: chaining prep, proving and baking times.
Understanding your results
Results can exceed 24 hours; this is a duration calculator, not a clock.
For the time between two clock times (09:15 to 17:40), use the time duration calculator.
Negative results mean the second time is longer than the first.
Tips and common mistakes
Treating minutes as decimals. 1.30 hours isn't 1 h 30 min.
Forgetting to carry when minutes exceed 59.
Mixing clock times and durations.
