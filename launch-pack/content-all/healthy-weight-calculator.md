H1: Healthy Weight Calculator
A "healthy weight" for adults is usually defined as the weight range that gives a body mass index (BMI) of 18.5 to 24.9 for your height. This calculator shows that range in kilograms and pounds. It's a quick starting point; your ideal weight within or near that range depends on your build, muscle mass, age and health.
How to use this calculator
1. Units: metric (cm) or imperial (ft and in).
2. Height: without shoes.
Results: healthy weight range in kg and in lb.
The formula
BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)², so rearranging:
Lower weight = 18.5 × Height (m)²
Upper weight = 24.9 × Height (m)²
In pounds, divide the kilogram values by 0.45359237.
Worked examples
Example 1: 175 cm
Height² = 1.75² = 3.0625
Healthy range = 56.7 to 76.3 kg (124.9 to 168.1 lb)
Example 2: 5 ft 6 in
5 ft 6 in = 66 in = 167.6 cm
Healthy range = 114.6 to 154.3 lb
Example 3: 162 cm
Healthy range = 48.6 to 65.3 kg

Where the 18.5 and 24.9 limits come from
The cut-offs were set by the World Health Organization in the late 1990s, based on studies linking BMI to the risk of ill health and death across large populations. The US National Institutes of Health adopted them in 1998, and Canada and the UK use the same values. They describe risk across a population, not a precise threshold for one person, which is why a weight a little above or below the range isn't automatically a problem.

Understanding your results
BMI is a screening tool. It doesn't measure body fat directly or show where fat is stored. Muscular people can have a high BMI without excess fat, and some people within the healthy range carry fat around the waist that raises health risks.
Waist size adds useful information. Keeping your waist to less than half your height is a simple guide used by NICE in the UK: for a 175 cm person, a waist under 87.5 cm. Try the waist-to-height ratio calculator.
Ethnicity: NICE recommends lower BMI thresholds for people of South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African or African-Caribbean family background, with overweight starting at 23 and obesity at 27.5. At 175 cm, a BMI of 23 corresponds to 70.4 kg.
Older adults: some research suggests slightly higher BMIs may be associated with lower risk in older age; discuss targets with your doctor.
Pregnancy: BMI ranges don't apply during pregnancy; see the pregnancy weight gain calculator.
If you're outside the range
Being outside the healthy range doesn't mean you're unhealthy, and being inside it doesn't guarantee good health. If you're above the range, even a 5% to 10% weight loss can improve blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol for people with excess weight. If you're below it, talk to a doctor about possible causes and whether you need to gain weight. The calorie deficit calculator and TDEE calculator can help with planning.
Country notes
United States: the CDC uses the categories above for adults 20+, and BMI-for-age percentiles for ages 2 to 19.
Canada: Health Canada's guidelines use the same adult BMI categories and recommend using waist circumference with BMI.
United Kingdom: the NHS uses the same categories, plus the lower thresholds for some ethnic groups and the waist-to-height ratio.
Tips and common mistakes
Measuring height with shoes on, which shifts the range.
Treating the top of the range as a goal when your doctor may suggest a different target.
Using adult ranges for children.
Frequently asked questions
What is a healthy weight for my height?
The range that gives a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9; this calculator shows it for your height.
Is BMI accurate?
It's a reasonable population screening tool but doesn't account for muscle, bone or fat distribution.
Does the range differ for men and women?
Adult BMI ranges are the same for both sexes.
What is a healthy weight for someone 5 ft 6 in?
About 115 to 154 lb.
Is the healthy range different for older people?
The standard categories still apply, but clinicians may consider other factors.
Should I aim for the middle of the range?
Not necessarily. Health markers, fitness and how you feel matter more than a specific number.
